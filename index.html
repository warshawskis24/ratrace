How it works

Toronto.

Beneath Toronto is a hidden world. Every day, thousands of people walk through the P.A.T.H. without noticing what's around them.

Secret passages.
Hidden landmarks.
Unexpected discoveries.

For one day only, the P.A.T.H. underground becomes Toronto's largest scavenger hunt. Navigate the tunnels as fast as you can — solving clues, uncovering hidden gems, and collecting bonus points as you go. Chase the glory of being crowned P.A.T.H. Champion. Hurry, the clock is ticking.
